What this project is

Diamond Operatore Player is a free, independent, non-commercial media player application that runs entirely in your web browser. It is a personal open-source project - not a company, not a business, and not a service.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no telemetry. The site never sends your data anywhere.
  • Your playlist URL and its parsed contents are processed entirely in your browser. Network requests go only to the provider address you yourself entered (plus one public CDN for the video-player library).
  • For convenience, the following is stored locally in your browser only: your playlist URL, a cached copy of the parsed playlist (so reloads are instant), and a small compatibility flag for your provider. Pressing Change Playlist erases all of it.
  • Playlist URLs may contain credentials; the application never logs them and never transmits them to anyone except your own provider.
